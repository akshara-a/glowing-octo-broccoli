Started at one farm gate in 2019

Nomad Roasters began when our founder bought forty kilos of green coffee from a single farm in Huila, Colombia, and roasted it in a popcorn machine on a balcony. That lot sold out in a weekend.

Six years later we still buy from named farms, still roast in small batches, and still print the whole story on the bag — we just have a proper roaster now.
